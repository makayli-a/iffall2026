Ghanaian Style Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is the pride of West Africa. This vibrant, one-pot dish is packed with bold flavors and a delightful kick that'll have you coming back for seconds.

Recipe Sources: Jaylynn Little, My African Food Map, Tasty

prep time~10-15 mins

cooking time~1 hr 10 mins

total time1 hr 30 mins

serving size6-8 people

EQUIPMENT

  • Blender
  • Large heavy bottom pot
  • Knife
  • Measuring cup
  • Spoon/tool for stirring
  • Foil
  • Fork
  • Serving dishes
  • Storage containers

INGREDIENTS

  • 6 large tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 4 large onions, roughly chopped
  • 6 cloves of pressed garlic
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 500g of beef, chicken or lamb (alt: mixed vegetables)
  • 2 ½ cups long grain rice, rinsed
  • 1 ½ cups water
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 2 chicken bouillon cubes
  • 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 scotch bonnet or habanero pepper (Leave out if you don't like spice)
  • ground black pepper and salt
  • ½ teaspoon mixed dried herbs

INSTRUCTIONS

Preparation

  1. Blend the tomato base: Cut the tomatoes, onions, and red bell pepper into chunks, discarding the bell pepper seeds. Add them to a blender with the deseeded scotch bonnet pepper, ginger, and ½ cup of water. Blend until smooth, then set aside.

Cooking

  1. Cook the sauce: Heat the vegetable oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Once hot, add in the tomato paste and mix until fully incorporated, dark, and fragrant. Pour in the blended tomato-pepper mixture and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mixture begins to reduce and deepen in color.

  2. Season and build flavor: Add the curry powder, nutmeg, thyme, smoked paprika, salt, pepper, and crushed bouillon cubes. Cook for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the stew thickens, reduces by half, and turns a deep red with oil beginning to separate on top.

  3. Add the rice and simmer: Stir in the rinsed rice, bay leaves, and chicken/beef broth. Bring the pot to a boil, then immediately reduce the heat to low. Cover tightly with foil and a lid, and simmer for 30-45 minutes until the rice is cooked through and the liquid is absorbed.

  4. Rest and serve: Remove from heat and let the rice rest, covered, for 5-7 minutes before fluffing with a fork.

  5. Enjoy! Serve warm then store in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Best for 5-7 days.
